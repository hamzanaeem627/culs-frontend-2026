Upload a photo. Get a skin check in seconds.

    1. Upload

      You attach a photo in the chat.

    FAQ

    Is this a medical diagnosis?

    No. DermaChat is a thesis prototype and gives an estimate only. Always consult a doctor.

    What happens to my photo?

    The photo is analysed on your device (or by the local prototype server) and is not uploaded anywhere else. A small thumbnail is kept in your browser so the chat survives a reload; Clear chat removes it.

    Which skin tones does it support?

    Fairness across skin tones is one of the thesis research questions.
